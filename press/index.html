Matt Jinn

I.

Bio

Matt Jinn is an independent singer-songwriter based in New York City making love songs at the intersection of pop R&B, indie folk, and chill pop. His bittersweet lyrics, warm production, and smooth tone have accumulated over 20,000 monthly listeners and 1 million streams across platforms, built entirely without a label or management team.

His debut EP Icarus (2026) captures the trajectory of a modern love story across six songs. His audience spans the US, Southeast Asia, and East Asia. He actively works with other artists and songwriters, most notably co-writing and featuring on “delusion(al)” with FION (500K+ streams across platforms).
